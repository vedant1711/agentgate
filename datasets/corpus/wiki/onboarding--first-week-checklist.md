# Onboarding: First Week Checklist

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles first week checklist within onboarding.

## Key facts
- The review cadence for first week checklist is 5 weeks.
- The platform lead owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 5 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the People Ops team if the process stalls for more than 10 business days.
