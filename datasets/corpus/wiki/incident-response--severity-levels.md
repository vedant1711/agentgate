# Incident Response: Severity Levels

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles severity levels within incident response.

## Key facts
- The turnaround time for severity levels is 1 business days.
- The finance controller owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 6 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the People Ops team if the process stalls for more than 12 business days.
