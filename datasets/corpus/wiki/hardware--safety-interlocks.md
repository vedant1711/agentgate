# Hardware: Safety Interlocks

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles safety interlocks within hardware.

## Key facts
- The retention window for safety interlocks is 36 days.
- The people ops partner owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 6 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Field Engineering team if the process stalls for more than 12 business days.
