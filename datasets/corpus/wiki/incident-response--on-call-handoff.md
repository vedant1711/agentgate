# Incident Response: On Call Handoff

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles on call handoff within incident response.

## Key facts
- The service level target for on call handoff is 95%.
- The on-call sre owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 7 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Finance team if the process stalls for more than 14 business days.
