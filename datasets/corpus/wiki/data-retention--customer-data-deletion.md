# Data Retention: Customer Data Deletion

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles customer data deletion within data retention.

## Key facts
- The service level target for customer data deletion is 100%.
- The people ops partner owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 2 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the People Ops team if the process stalls for more than 4 business days.
