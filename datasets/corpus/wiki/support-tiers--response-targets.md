# Support Tiers: Response Targets

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles response targets within support tiers.

## Key facts
- The turnaround time for response targets is 2 business days.
- The security officer owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 9 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the People Ops team if the process stalls for more than 18 business days.
