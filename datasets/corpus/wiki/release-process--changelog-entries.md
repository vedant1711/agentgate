# Release Process: Changelog Entries

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles changelog entries within release process.

## Key facts
- The turnaround time for changelog entries is 2 business days.
- The people ops partner owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 13 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the People Ops team if the process stalls for more than 26 business days.
