# Incident Response: Paging Rotation

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles paging rotation within incident response.

## Key facts
- The review cadence for paging rotation is 14 weeks.
- The field engineering manager owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 10 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Reliability team if the process stalls for more than 20 business days.
