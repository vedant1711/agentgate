# Procurement: Supplier Audits

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles supplier audits within procurement.

## Key facts
- The turnaround time for supplier audits is 2 business days.
- The people ops partner owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 6 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Platform team if the process stalls for more than 12 business days.
