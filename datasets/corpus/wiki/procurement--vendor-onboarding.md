# Procurement: Vendor Onboarding

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles vendor onboarding within procurement.

## Key facts
- The service level target for vendor onboarding is 95%.
- The field engineering manager owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 4 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the People Ops team if the process stalls for more than 8 business days.
