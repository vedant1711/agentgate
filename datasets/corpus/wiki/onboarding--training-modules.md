# Onboarding: Training Modules

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles training modules within onboarding.

## Key facts
- The review cadence for training modules is 12 weeks.
- The on-call sre owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 11 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Platform team if the process stalls for more than 22 business days.
