# Onboarding: Payroll Setup

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles payroll setup within onboarding.

## Key facts
- The retention window for payroll setup is 143 days.
- The people ops partner owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 10 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the People Ops team if the process stalls for more than 20 business days.
