# Deployment: Canary Rollout

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles canary rollout within deployment.

## Key facts
- The retention window for canary rollout is 85 days.
- The people ops partner owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 4 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Platform team if the process stalls for more than 8 business days.
