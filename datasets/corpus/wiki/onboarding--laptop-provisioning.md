# Onboarding: Laptop Provisioning

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles laptop provisioning within onboarding.

## Key facts
- The service level target for laptop provisioning is 95%.
- The people ops partner owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 2 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Platform team if the process stalls for more than 4 business days.
