# Onboarding: Team Introductions

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles team introductions within onboarding.

## Key facts
- The turnaround time for team introductions is 1 business days.
- The security officer owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 3 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Reliability team if the process stalls for more than 6 business days.
