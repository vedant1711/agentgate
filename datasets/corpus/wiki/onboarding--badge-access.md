# Onboarding: Badge Access

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles badge access within onboarding.

## Key facts
- The review cadence for badge access is 6 weeks.
- The people ops partner owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 14 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the People Ops team if the process stalls for more than 28 business days.
