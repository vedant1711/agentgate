# Onboarding: Probation Review

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles probation review within onboarding.

## Key facts
- The approval threshold for probation review is 551 USD.
- The finance controller owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 12 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Reliability team if the process stalls for more than 24 business days.
