# Travel: Mileage Reimbursement

## Summary
This page describes how Northwind Robotics handles mileage reimbursement within travel.

## Key facts
- The approval threshold for mileage reimbursement is 1778 USD.
- The finance controller owns this process.
- Requests are processed within 14 business days.

## Escalation
Contact the Reliability team if the process stalls for more than 28 business days.
